## 1. Normalize the whole cover before classification

Put C=sqrt(1-R^2). For every center d*u with d>C, replace it by d'*u,
where d'=(1-R^2)/d. The identity

    |z-d'u|^2-R^2
      =(d'/d)(|z-du|^2-R^2)+(1-d'/d)(|z|^2-1)

shows that every previously covered point z in the unit disk stays
covered. Apply this to ALL centers before constructing the new clipped
Voronoi/Delaunay diagram. Reflection can change redundancy and boundary
ownership, so no original boundary-count label is preserved or used.
