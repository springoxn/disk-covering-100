## 1. Normalize every center before classifying the diagram

Put C=sqrt(1-R^2). For a center d*u with d>C, replace it by d'*u, where
d'=(1-R^2)/d. The exact identity

    |z-d'u|^2-R^2
      =(d'/d)(|z-du|^2-R^2)+(1-d'/d)(|z|^2-1)

preserves its covered subset of the target disk. Apply this to ALL
centers before constructing the clipped Voronoi/straight-center diagram.
Redundancy and boundary ownership may change; no earlier count is kept.

constants are freshly checked. They exclude m<=99 and leave precisely
m100 with B31,...,39. The following branches exhaust those integers.
Every normalized branch is replayed at R itself, with the same norm cap.
