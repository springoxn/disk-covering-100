\section{The accepted interval and its history}

\[
\boxed{\ 0.113235\ <\ \rD(100)\ \le\ 0.1154469548855829\ }

$(U_\star-R_\star)/U_\star$ is about $1.92\%$ (display value).

\section{From $0.11323$ to $0.1132301$: the transplant and the first advance}

Stage 139 switched the programme from interface consolidation back to radius advancement. It
replayed the absorbed six-branch skeleton inside an isolated working copy (exit $0$, $626$ files
bound, source and copy hashes unchanged after the run), and produced a six-endpoint rational radius
scan ($0.11323$, $0.113231$, $0.113235$, $0.11324$, $0.11325$, $0.1133$) recording, for each
endpoint, the counting bridge, the $B31$ fixed-fee rows and the tail's conditional total budget.
Two facts emerged:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
\item At $R=0.11324$ the $99$-disk exclusion still holds, the necessary boundary counts for $100$
disks are still $31..39$, and the $B31$ final row, ear/bad-face signs, degree budget and fan
necessary restrictions all pass. At $R=0.11325$ the $B31$ fan premise no longer closes, so the whole
branch fails there even though its final area margin is still positive.
\item With the old tail constants the total budget allows radii only up to a threshold in
\[
\Bigl[\frac{11323679462887}{10^{14}},\ \frac{113236794628871}{10^{15}}\Bigr),
\]
and at $R=0.11324$ the old tail budget is about $-0.000160907$. Holding $\mu$ and $\lambda$ fixed,
the intercept would have to drop below about $0.007505530351154887$.
\end{itemize}

The $B31$ arithmetic was then replayed explicitly at $R=11324/100000$

the proposal) adapted. The certificate lists the geometric transfer obligations separately from the
arithmetic replay, which is the honest way to record that ``the arithmetic still closes'' is not the

\section{The $B=36$ obstruction, and why the obvious repairs fail}

\subsection{The exact obstruction}

At $R=2831/25000$ with the frozen constants ($\lambda=3/50$, $\mu=92531/10^{7}$, marked constant
$K=973/3125$, old intercept $L=1501/200000$) the rational point
\[
x=y=\frac{9921033}{12500000}
\]
has a pair relaxation whose interval is
$[3754996055077/5\cdot10^{14},\,3754996055079/5\cdot10^{14}]$. Its lower endpoint exceeds the old
intercept by $2496055077/5\cdot10^{14}\approx4.99211\times10^{-6}$ and exceeds the $B=36$
area-allowed maximum intercept by
$16062333091071696817530221032068394059542879425773781/(36\cdot10^{55})$. The obstruction certificate
therefore returns \texttt{PASS\_\allowbreak{}OBSTRUCTION\_\allowbreak{}TO\_\allowbreak{}OLD\_\allowbreak{}RELAXATION} with
\texttt{full\_\allowbreak{}B36plus\_\allowbreak{}closed = false} and \texttt{global\_\allowbreak{}bound\_\allowbreak{}improved = false}, and its scope
string states that this is not a covering configuration and not a counterexample to the target
lower bound.

\subsection{Four repairs that do not work}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{More boxes.} The obstruction is a point-wise inequality at an exact rational point; the
pair value there is what it is, so subdivision cannot lower it. A later exact diagnostic confirmed
that both support terms vanish at the obstruction point
(\texttt{n100-\allowbreak{}tail-\allowbreak{}point-\allowbreak{}branch-\allowbreak{}212.json},
\texttt{n100-\allowbreak{}tail-\allowbreak{}direct-\allowbreak{}components-\allowbreak{}216.json}), so refining the support boxes is mathematically
insufficient.
\item \textbf{A higher intercept.} Raising $L$ makes the $B=36$ budget negative, because the budget
already fails at the old intercept.
\item \textbf{Reusing the $B\ge37$ intercept.} The $B\ge37$ certificate closes with $L=19/2500$; using
it at $B=36$ makes that budget negative.
\item \textbf{Synchronized retuning of slope and penalty.} A broader discovery search over the
direct pair/angle family produced the optimum
\[
\lambda=0.05944697773645484,\quad \mu=0.010360567875057177,\quad
K\approx0.31268174443157726,
\]
with a local peak of about $0.00739518789840696$ against a $B=36$ allowed intercept of about
$0.007391936425058569$: a per-cell deficit of about $-3.25147334839\times10^{-6}$ and a total $B=36$
deficit of about $-1.17053040542\times10^{-4}$. This agrees with an independent search in the same
family. So within the frozen direct pair/angle relaxation, simple synchronized retuning cannot close
$B=36$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What was actually tried and stopped}

Two rational candidates were started and did not finish:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
\item $R=113237/10^{6}$ with $L=75097/10^{7}$: the scalar budget is positive
(margin $4.906445222464608\times10^{-7}$, gain $5.1549192230158705\times10^{-3}$), and a
$101$-point rational scan between $1132301/10^{7}$ and $2831/25000$ showed that the known
obstruction point stays within budget up to the exact sample
$R=11323703/100000000=0.11323703$ and strictly exceeds it from the exact sample
$R=113237129/1000000000$ onwards ($30$ of the $101$ samples are obstructed by that one point). The
scan's own scope string says it is diagnostic only: a sample not obstructed by this point is not a
global $B36$ certificate. The full pair-domain replay near the budget edge subdivided for too long
and was stopped.
\item $R=113236/10^{6}$ with $L=0.007511$ and, more conservatively, $L=0.00751$: both stopped for the
same reason.
\end{itemize}

Neither is reported as a failure and neither is a certificate. The conservative candidate
$R=22647/200000=0.113235$ with $L=7512/1000000=939/125000$ has a much wider budget margin and did
complete: $1\,841\,089$ boxes visited, $917\,297$ accepted, the three $q$-supports passing with
$3761/104/35$ boxes, and both the $B=36$ margin and the per-$B$ gain strictly positive. That is the
candidate which was accepted, and it is the reason the frontier sits at $0.113235$.

\begin{remark}[The undecided window, stated precisely]
The verifiable undecided window is $(0.113235,\,0.11324]$. Inside it, the fixed-old-parameter
single-point scan indicates that from $113237129/1000000000$ onwards that particular obstruction
point alone blocks the old relaxation --- but this is a statement about one relaxation at one point,
\emph{not} a mathematical upper bound on all new routes.
\end{remark}

\section{Three hard boundaries that the route catalogue respects}

The systematic catalogue of Parts VI makes three boundaries explicit, and they are worth stating
before the catalogue itself:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=3pt]
\item The recursive inner-disk counts ($\ge56$, then $\ge58$ inner disks, hence $k\le44$ and then
$k\le42$ boundary-serving disks) are consequences of a counting argument, \emph{not} new global
radius bounds. Throughout all of those stages the global interval was unchanged.
\item The progression $0.11516023\to0.11521651$ is a threshold advance \emph{inside} the conditional
branch $k\ge55$. The complementary branch $k\le54$ was never excluded along that line, and one stage
even produced an explicit integer feasible point of the aggregate relaxation
($34$ near-end $+$ $20$ tail-end $+$ $46$ non-serving $=100$ disks with $54$ serving), which
forbids any logical consequence of that relaxation from being empty.
\item Every \texttt{PASS} in the interface/contract stages means contract registration, replay
consistency or a timing sample --- never a geometric closure. All $174$ legacy sources are
\texttt{PRECONDITION\_\allowbreak{}MISSING}, and the dependency gate is \texttt{UNDECIDED}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What a genuine advance would require}

The frontier is not blocked by compute; it is blocked by the strength of one inequality. The
certificates and diagnostics point to the same conclusion:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{A new geometric inequality for $B=36$}: a common-endpoint lens constraint, or a
genuinely coupled multi-edge averaging inequality, replacing the direct pair/angle relaxation. A
rewrite that merely re-expresses a term already present in the pair expression adds no information
--- this was tested explicitly (replacing a coarse $4\lambda R^2$ distance cap by the exact centre
distance gives nothing new, because that term is already in the original expression).
\item \textbf{A resumable pair-box queue}: persist the pending boxes per round, bind parameters by
hash, cache inverse-trigonometric and local-support intervals, and shard the domain into diagonal and
off-diagonal regions with multiple processes. The stopped runs at $0.113236$ and $0.113237$ failed on
wall-clock, not on mathematics; without a queue, each new candidate restarts from the root.
\item \textbf{A sharper universal bound}, if the engine is to be bypassed: the configuration-free
best is $0.11148$, which is $1.55\%$ below the accepted engine bound, so this is not a near-term
substitute.
\item \textbf{The exact-value route}: a complete enumeration of the internal map family, a treatment
of $m<100$, degenerate configurations, and a closure of both bounds onto the same algebraic number,
followed by irreducibility and root isolation. The square-relaxation / real-quantifier-elimination
theory absorbed from the third-party material is the only known terminating route to an exact value,
but it has no working $n=100$ backend: the zero set of the compiled polynomial system is singular
everywhere, so a regular Krawczyk box cannot replace a full QE backend, and the variable budget
($605$ geometric $+$ $1318$ relaxation $=1923$, with $1922$ eliminated) is a budget, not a runtime.
\end{itemize}
