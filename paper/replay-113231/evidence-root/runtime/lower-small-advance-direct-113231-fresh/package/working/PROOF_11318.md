## 1. Normalize the entire cover before counting boundary cells

Set R=5659/50000. Suppose a radius-at-most-R cover exists and enlarge its
disks to R if needed. Put C=sqrt(1-R^2). Apply the following operation to
ALL100 centers, before constructing or classifying a covering diagram.
For a center d*u with d>C, replace it by d'*u where d'=(1-R^2)/d; keep
the other centers unchanged. For every target point z,

    |z-d'u|^2-R^2
      =(d'/d)(|z-du|^2-R^2)+(1-d'/d)(|z|^2-1).

Thus each replacement preserves every covered point of the unit disk.
The resulting cover has every center of norm at most C. Reflection may
change nearest-site ownership, redundancy, and the boundary-cell count.
We therefore form a NEW complete clipped Voronoi/Delaunay diagram only
after normalizing all centers. Every m and B below belongs to that same
normalized diagram, not to the original unnormalized one.
