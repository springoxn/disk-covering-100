## 1. Normalize all centers before constructing the diagram

Set C=sqrt(1-R^2). Replace every center d*u with d>C by d'*u,
where d'=(1-R^2)/d. For every point z in the unit disk,

    |z-d'u|^2-R^2
      =(d'/d)(|z-du|^2-R^2)+(1-d'/d)(|z|^2-1).

Thus coverage is preserved. Apply this operation to ALL centers before
forming the clipped Voronoi/Delaunay diagram. Reflection may change
redundancy, nearest-site ownership and the boundary count; all counts
