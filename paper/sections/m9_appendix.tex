\subsection{The obstruction}

At $R=2831/25000$ with $\lambda=3/50$, $\mu=92531/10^{7}$, marked constant $K=973/3125$ and old
intercept $L=1501/200000$, the point $x=y=9921033/12500000$ satisfies
\[
\frac{3754996055077}{500000000000000}\ \le\ \text{pair}\ \le\
\frac{3754996055079}{500000000000000},
\]
so
\[
\text{pair}-L\ \ge\ \frac{2496055077}{500000000000000}\approx4.99211\times10^{-6},
\]
and the $B=36$ area budget permits at most
\[
L_{\max}=\frac{27019909263463328303182469778967931605940457120574226219}
{3600000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000},
\]
giving an excess of
\[
\frac{16062333091071696817530221032068394059542879425773781}
{3600000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000}>0 .
\]
The direct-term decomposition at the same point is
\[
\lambda\text{-distance}\in\Bigl[\frac{1666193587123}{10^{15}},\frac{416548396781}{250000000000000}\Bigr],
\quad
\tfrac12H\in\Bigl[\frac{3338279809719}{500000000000000},\frac{83456995243}{12500000000000}\Bigr],

\qquad
\frac{\text{distance}}{R^2}=\frac{27769893118719}{12823297600000}.
\]

\section{Audit record for this document}

The following checks were executed while preparing this monograph, and their results are reported in
the text rather than only in this appendix:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=3pt]
\item \texttt{python reproduce.py} --- exit $0$; accepted bound $0.113235$; $0.11324$ retained as
not closed; four certificate hashes printed (Part VII, the entry-point subsection).
\item \textbf{Artifact-coverage audit} --- of $100$ paths referenced by the mirror's own documents,
$19$ exist in the mirror and $81$ do not; $80$ of the $81$ exist in the parent repository;
\texttt{n100-\allowbreak{}public/\allowbreak{}proofs/\allowbreak{}} is absent while the referenced proof exists in the parent repository
with a SHA-256 matching the certificate's \texttt{proof\_\allowbreak{}sha256} (Part VII, the artifact-coverage subsection).
\item \textbf{Binding audit} --- the independent lattice receipt binds a stale input and proof
(\texttt{7e732e18\ldots} and \texttt{a0454013\ldots}) while its checker binding
(\texttt{9655d038\ldots}) matches the current script (Part VII, the binding-audit subsection).
\item \textbf{Numeric re-derivation} --- the scalar budget formula was re-evaluated in exact rational
arithmetic at both candidate radii and reproduces the stored margins and gains exactly
(Appendix~\ref{app:budget}).
\item \textbf{Fraction-digit audit} --- the $B=36$ margins are numerators of $52$ digits over
$10^{56}$; the obstruction's area-allowed intercept is a $56$-digit numerator over $36\cdot10^{55}$;
the covering squared-radius bound is a $31$-digit numerator over $10^{33}$ equal to $U_\star^2$.
\item \textbf{Transplant-package check} --- the four sources cited by the branch certificates are
present in the parent repository's transplant package and absent from the mirror.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{remark}
This appendix deliberately lists the failed and retracted items alongside the accepted ones. A
record that only ever confirms itself has not been tested; the value of the list above is that it
shows where the programme's own machinery was wrong and what replaced it.
\end{remark}
