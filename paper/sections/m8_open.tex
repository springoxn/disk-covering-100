\section{What is established, precisely}

For the avoidance of doubt, the strongest statements this dossier can support are:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{An upper bound.} There is an explicit $100$-centre rational configuration whose
covering radius is at most $U_\star=1154469548855829/10^{16}=0.1154469548855829$, certified by
$138\,437$ exact integer objects ($128\,436$ interior triples, $9\,900$ boundary pairs, $100$
antipodal pairs, $1$ exceptional case), with the exact covering squared-radius bound
$1332799939235381342323027277241/10^{33}=U_\star^2$, improving the reference value
$115446954885583/10^{15}$ by exactly $1/10^{16}$.
\item \textbf{A lower bound.} $B31$--$B35$ are excluded at $R=2831/25000$ by exact certificates;
$B\ge37$ is excluded at $R=2831/25000$ with intercept $19/2500$; $B=36$ is excluded at
$R_\star=22647/200000$ with intercept $939/125000$ over $1\,841\,089$ rational boxes. Radius
monotonicity then gives $B31$--$B35$ and $B\ge37$ at $R_\star$ as well, so
\[
\rD(100)>22647/200000=0.113235 .
\]
\item \textbf{Configuration-free bounds.} $\rD(100)\ge2787/25000=0.11148$ (long chord), with
$\ge1111720153/10^{10}$ (sharpened envelope) and $\ge554570693/5000000000$ (integer edge counting)
also valid.
\item \textbf{An exact obstruction.} With the frozen capped-pair constants, the $B=36$ relaxation at
$R=2831/25000$ exceeds both the old intercept and the area-allowed maximum intercept at the rational
point $x=y=9921033/12500000$. This is a statement about a relaxation, not about coverings.
\end{enumerate}

Everything else is either conditional, diagnostic, interface-level, or open.

\section{Claims that were withdrawn or corrected}

A research record is only credible if it keeps its retractions. The following claims were made and
then withdrawn or corrected, and the corrections are part of the record:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{The $4\arcsin(r/2)$ arc bound.} Wrong; the correct maximum angular measure of
$\partial D\cap B(c_i,r)$ is $2\arcsin r$, attained at $\|c_i\|=\sqrt{1-r^2}$. The wrong constant
cannot imply $<\!\pi$ near $r=1$ and is unusable.
\item \textbf{The boundary-annulus ``global no-gain'' acceptance.} Voided: a wrong inner-face filter
made the pending set empty, and the independent script had duplicated the same error. Exit code $0$
is not evidence that the coverage domain is right.
\item \textbf{The stage-47 v2 exclusion of all $4776$ configurations.} Rejected.
\item \textbf{The angular-energy inertia claim $+25/-6$.} Retracted: CAS on the real data gives one
negative direction in the $32\times32$ angle coordinates (plus one zero = global rotation), and one
negative after projection to the $31$-dimensional gap coordinates. No inertia claim is made any more.
\item \textbf{``The radial-shell LP gives a certified covering-number bound near $92.56$.''}
Corrected: the sampled solution is infeasible on a $4001$-point fine grid (coverage $0.99903<1$,
dual $1.00317>1$); the only certified statement is the coarse bracket
$[1/r^2,\ \pi/\mathrm{lens}(1,r)]=[80.911,\,165.734]$ at $r=L$. The stronger phrasing ``the route is
closed / a certificate is impossible'' was also withdrawn.
\item \textbf{``The configuration side still has $5$--$6\%$ of room.''} Withdrawn: the small-$n$
optima are ring-type families while the best $n=100$ configuration is a lattice-type family, so they
are not comparable; the lattice-family ratio $c(n)$ is $2.13$ ($n=40$), $1.92$ ($50$), $1.35$ ($70$),
$1.14$ ($100$), $1.12$ ($140$), $0.82$ ($200$).
\item \textbf{Stage-142 discovery numbers} (an ear approximation of $1.332634$ against a true
$1.42016417919847$, and a $K$-objective erratum) must not be quoted as certificate values.
\item \textbf{The stage-44 first version} ($4496$ contractions / $597$ incidences) is superseded by
$2248/562$; \textbf{stage-45/46} used the wrong outer-dart owner; and a historical script hash
mismatch in the dependency audit was \emph{diagnosed}, not silently repaired.
\end{itemize}

\section{The honest reading of the gap}

Two numbers frame the situation. The configuration-free best lower bound is $0.11148$, which is
$1.55\%$ below the engine's accepted bound $0.113235$; and the accepted interval is
$[0.113235,\,0.1154469548855829]$, a relative width of about $1.92\%$. The engine is therefore much
stronger than any single analytic inequality available, and the remaining work is not a matter of
more precision or more boxes: it is one inequality for one branch, at one radius, plus --- for the
exact value --- an enumeration and an elimination backend that do not yet exist at $n=100$.

It is equally important to say what the current bound is \emph{not} sensitive to. Adding boxes,
raising an intercept, reusing the $B\ge37$ intercept, or synchronously retuning the slope and penalty
within the frozen family all fail for provable reasons (Part V). The bound is also not limited by
compute: the $B=36$ candidate run takes $130$ s of reported time, and the largest single cost in the
whole engine is the tail subdivision. What limits it is that the current local pair expression, at
the obstruction point, is genuinely larger than what the $B=36$ area budget can pay.
